# Close Calendar and Flux Analysis Policy

**Owner:** Corporate Accounting
**Applies to:** All NorthPeak entities
**Effective:** 2026-01-01 · Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the standard month-end close timetable and the flux
(variance) thresholds that determine which account movements require
written commentary in the close package.

## 2. Close Calendar

| Business day | Milestone |
| --- | --- |
| BD 1 | Prior-period accruals reverse. Bank reconciliation begins. |
| BD 2 | Department accrual submissions due, 6:00 PM CT (see Accrual Policy). |
| BD 3 | Subledger tie-outs completed: inventory, intercompany confirmations. |
| BD 4 | Flux analysis run; commentary drafted for all breaching accounts. |
| BD 5 | Close package assembled; controller review and sign-off. |

A submission that has not arrived by its calendar deadline is reported as
an open close item, by department, until it lands. The close does not wait
silently: open items are named in the BD 3 status update.

## 3. Journal Entry Standards

- Every journal must balance (total debits equal total credits) before it
  posts.
- Every journal carries a memo stating the business purpose and the
  supporting document or reference. A journal posted without support is a
  control exception and is reported in the close package.
- Duplicate postings of the same transaction are prohibited. The BD 4
  review includes a duplicate scan on entity, account, vendor, and amount.

## 4. Flux Thresholds

Commentary is required for any entity-account whose movement breaches
**both** tests, against budget **or** against the prior month:

1. Percentage: change of **10% or more**, and
2. Magnitude: change of **$5 million or more**.

Breaches against either comparison (budget, prior month) trigger the
requirement. Accounts below both tests require no commentary, though
reviewers may request it for unusual items at any size.

## 5. Commentary Standard

Each required commentary states, in order:

1. **Driver** - what actually caused the movement, named specifically
   (vendor, project, event), not "increased spend."
2. **Amount** - the dollar and percentage change.
3. **Character** - one-time or recurring.
4. **Outlook** - whether the movement is expected to continue, grow, or
   reverse next period.

Draft commentary is prepared for the close package and takes effect only
on controller approval.
