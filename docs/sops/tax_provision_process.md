# Tax Provision Process

**Owner:** Corporate Tax
**Applies to:** All NorthPeak entities
**Effective:** 2026-01-01 · Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

This process governs how each entity computes its quarterly income tax
provision and how the consolidated provision is assembled, reviewed, and
explained.

## 2. Entity Submissions

- Each entity submits, for the quarter: pretax income and its provision
  computation, by **business day 4** of the close following quarter end.
- The submission states the effective rate applied and reconciles it to
  the statutory rate (permanent differences, credits, and state taxes
  itemized).

## 3. Consolidation

- The consolidated provision is the sum of the entity provisions,
  assembled by Corporate Tax at the parent.
- Consolidation is a roll-up, not a netting exercise: entity detail is
  preserved in the provision package so any entity's contribution to the
  consolidated total - and to its change - can be traced.

## 4. Change Analysis

- Any entity whose provision changes by **$25 million or more** quarter
  over quarter must provide a written driver explanation.
- The expected drivers, in the order they are checked:
  1. **Pretax income movement** - the provision moved because earnings did.
  2. **Rate effects** - law changes, mix of state or foreign earnings.
  3. **Discrete items** - settlements, valuation allowance changes,
     return-to-provision adjustments.
- Corporate Tax sanity-checks each entity's implied effective rate against
  its pretax income; a provision that moves materially more or less than
  pretax income, without a rate or discrete explanation, is returned to
  the entity before consolidation.
