# Professional Services Engagement Policy

**Owner:** Corporate Finance
**Applies to:** All NorthPeak entities
**Effective:** 2026-01-01 · Version 1.0

## 1. Purpose

Professional services (account **6100**) is one of the company's largest
controllable expense lines and one of its most variable. This policy
governs how outside consulting and professional engagements are approved,
recorded, and explained, so that spend in this account is never a surprise
at close.

## 2. Approval Thresholds

| Engagement size | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Under $1 million | Entity finance approval; statement of work on file |
| $1 million or more | Corporate Finance approval and a signed SOW **before work begins** |
| $5 million or more | Written business case (need, alternatives considered, expected outcome) plus Corporate Finance approval |

Work that begins before its required approval is a policy exception and
is reported in the close package, regardless of the engagement's merit.

## 3. Recording

- All engagements are coded to account 6100, with the vendor name and the
  engagement or SOW reference in the journal memo.
- Engagements are recorded by the entity that receives the service, not
  the entity that pays, where the two differ.

## 4. Monitoring and Explanation

- Corporate Finance reports 6100 spend versus budget, by entity, every
  month as part of the close.
- Because 6100 is a flux-monitored account, any entity whose 6100 movement
  breaches the flux thresholds (see Close Calendar and Flux Analysis
  Policy) explains the movement by naming the engagements that drove it -
  vendor, purpose, and amount - in the flux commentary.
- Recurring "close support" and staff-augmentation engagements are named
  as such in commentary; they are not described as project work.
