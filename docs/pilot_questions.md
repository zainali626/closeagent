# Pilot question set - REBUILT 2026-11-15 from month-end close research
# (Ledge 2025 close benchmarks; practitioner close literature). The old
# hand-picked set was scrapped at Zain's direction; every question below
# maps to a documented close pain, noted in brackets. Amounts are at
# Koch-conglomerate scale (~$115B revenue) per the dataset design.

The agent must handle these at month-end close, in close order:

1. Which October bank transactions don't match the books, and how much is still unexplained? [bank reconciliation - the #1 close time sink, 20-50 hrs/month]
2. What are we still waiting on to finish October's close - which submissions haven't arrived? [dependency on other departments - the #1 close blocker, 56%]
3. Did September's accruals reverse properly, or is anything double-counted in October? [unreversed accruals duplicating costs]
4. Which October costs are still unaccrued, and what does policy say we book for invoices that arrive after cutoff? [missed accruals / late information]
5. Do all October journals balance, are any duplicated, and was anything posted without support? [journal entry control]
6. Do the entities agree on their intercompany balances? Which pair is off, and by how much? [intercompany differences]
7. Did the inventory subledger tie to the GL at close, and which locations did not reconcile? [subledger-to-GL tie-out - the Sagent finance inventory close pattern]
8. Which accounts breached the flux threshold versus last month and versus budget? [flux / variance analysis]
9. Why is professional services 22% over budget in October? [variance drill-down]
10. Draft the flux commentary for account 6100 for the close package. [commentary drafting + human approval]
11. What's the consolidated tax provision for the quarter, and which entities drove the change versus last quarter? [multi-entity consolidation - the Koch pattern]
12. Explain the COGS variance for the Dallas region. [variance analysis / cost drivers]

Dropped from the old set: AR balance by channel and top-vendor spend -
the weakest matches to documented close pain.
